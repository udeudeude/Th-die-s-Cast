# Ruins and Relics

## Production brief
Each numbered entry is an independent, system-neutral encounter. Numbers and headings are production metadata, never spoken. A listener seeks into the encounter portion, ignores the fragment underway, and uses the next encounter heard from its beginning. A discreet neutral cue marks each boundary. Do not prescribe combat, rewards, or an explanation. Suggested production: Brother Rubicon, one quiet introductory observation, then “I am Brother Rubicon. The die's cast.” with a 0.3-second beat on either side of that complete sentence. No fixed-duration claim until the narration is rendered and measured. The ending is not an encounter.

**Episode identity:** Discoveries in abandoned places, each with a physical clue and an unresolved invitation.
**Opening observation:** Old stones keep their counsel. Sometimes, however, they repeat themselves.
**Closing coda (not an encounter):** There was another room on the plan. I cannot remember who erased it.

## Encounter manuscript (20 independent results)

### 01 | The Second Foundation
Excavators have uncovered foundations beneath a ruined chapel. The lower walls follow the same plan, except that every doorway opens toward the altar. The foreman has begun finding fresh mortar on his tools each morning.

### 02 | A Bell Without a Tower
A bronze bell rests half-buried in a field of fallen masonry. Its clapper is missing. Whenever someone approaches, a distant bell answers from underground, precisely one note lower.

### 03 | The Guest Register
In a roofless inn, a ledger lies open on a dry desk. The newest entry is dated tomorrow and lists the travelers by descriptions rather than names. The room numbers correspond to doors that no longer exist.

### 04 | The King's Empty Chair
A throne survives in a hall otherwise reduced to rubble. A line of dust-free footprints leads to it, but none lead away. The chair is still warm, and a crown-shaped shadow lies across its seat.

### 05 | The School of Silence
Stone desks stand in an abandoned school. Every slate bears the same unfinished question. An elderly passerby offers the answer, then begs the travelers not to write it down.

### 06 | The Unbroken Window
A single pane of stained glass hangs in the air where a monastery wall once stood. Through it, the ruin appears complete and inhabited. A figure on the other side keeps gesturing for the travelers to move aside.

### 07 | A Door for the Small
Among the ruins of a fortress is a doorway scarcely knee-high, reinforced with iron. On its lintel is a warning addressed to giants. Something inside is trying to push a full-sized table through.

### 08 | The Borrowed Staircase
A freestanding staircase rises to an absent second floor. Footsteps descend whenever the moon passes behind a cloud. At the bottom step, someone has left a pair of shoes filled with rainwater.

### 09 | The Clockmaker's Yard
Dozens of stone sundials surround a collapsed workshop. All cast shadows toward different hours. One shows the current time exactly, though the sun has already set.

### 10 | The Last Mosaic
A mosaic depicts the ruin as it stands today, down to a fallen pillar and the travelers' arrival. One tile shows a person kneeling to remove a different tile. That piece is loose.

### 11 | The Drowned Library
At the bottom of a dry cistern are shelves of books, all swollen with water. The pages are damp but the shelves are dusty. From the lowest shelf comes the sound of someone turning a page.

### 12 | The Missing Corner
A watchtower has three corners and a clean vertical gap where the fourth should be. Looking through the gap reveals a different landscape. Birds flying across it briefly disappear.

### 13 | The Foundry's Breath
An abandoned foundry furnace exhales warm air at regular intervals. A metalworker insists the furnace has been cold for a century. Its ash contains an immaculate bronze key.

### 14 | The Statue's Repair
A toppled statue has been carefully mended with fresh linen bandages rather than mortar. Its face is missing. At dusk, a person arrives carrying a bowl of water and asks whether the patient has spoken.

### 15 | The Theater Beneath
Under a ruined amphitheater, a stage is intact and set for a banquet. A lone stagehand adjusts the chairs. They say the audience has arrived early, although no performance is scheduled.

### 16 | The Road in the Crypt
A crypt's stone floor contains a short paved road, complete with milestones. The inscriptions give distances to places not on any map. One milestone is newly painted and points straight down.

### 17 | The Garden of Keys
Keys grow from the branches of a thorn tree in a courtyard abandoned generations ago. Each is tagged with a room name. The gardener refuses to say whether the rooms are in this building.

### 18 | The Bridge's Memory
Only the piers of an ancient bridge remain. A ferry operator insists the bridge still carries travelers every winter. Fresh hoofprints cross the water's surface and stop at the far bank.

### 19 | The House with Two Histories
Two neighboring ruins bear identical plaques describing the same family, fire, and date. Each plaque accuses the other house of causing the disaster. A third plaque has been set aside, face down.

### 20 | The Unfinished Arch
A mason works alone on an arch in a deserted city. The stone at its summit is visibly too large to fit. She says the arch has been waiting for someone willing to stand beneath it.
