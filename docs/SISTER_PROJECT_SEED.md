# Sister project seed — original tabletop games played through audio

_Status: idea reservoir; intentionally separate from Th’die’s Cast utility episode production._

## Why a sister project?

Th’die’s Cast is primarily a podcast collection of **utilities for existing games**: dice and randomizers, timers, encounter tables, soundscapes, and audio props. A distinct creative opportunity is **games whose primary mechanical interface is an audio recording**, rather than a recording merely assisting another game.

This idea deserves its own design thread and probably its own repository, to avoid distracting the utility podcast roadmap.

## Foundational premise

Explore original, practical tabletop/party/solo games that require no downloaded proprietary app, can operate via a standard podcast episode, and make meaningful use of:
- starting at random points in a recorded stream;
- deliberate silence, timing and timed interruption;
- audio clues and deduction;
- spoken procedural generation and nonrepeating encounters;
- a narrator/moderator that may address or manipulate players;
- sound as a hidden information channel;
- lightweight optional browser interfaces when audio-only mechanics cannot work by themselves.

Set feasibility constraints: small downloads, mobile-first usability, offline playback when episode downloaded, no player screen dependence where avoidable, accessibility (transcripts/alternatives), rights-cleared original audio.

## Initial candidate mechanics

1. **A sound-deduction pursuit game**, borrowing only the broad idea of invisible movement inferred from audio clues, with original rules, sounds, and narrative.
2. **An unpredictable wizard referee**, whose episode interrupts gameplay with deterministic-time or seek-dependent events.
3. **An exploration oracle game**, where episodes present unique scenes and player choices interpret what happens next.
4. **Audio timer-driven co-op**, whose changing instructions form the game, not merely a countdown.
5. **A game playable from a single long MP3**, exploiting chapter marks, purposeful pauses, and arbitrary seeking.

## Thread-starter prompt

I want to start a **new, separate sister project** inspired by my utility podcast Th’die’s Cast (https://github.com/udeudeude/Th-die-s-Cast). That podcast will focus on tools for existing tabletop games: narrated dice, timers, encounter generators, and soundscapes. **This thread is different:** let's invent *original games designed specifically to be played using podcast/audio tracks*.

Constraints and goals:
- Make audio a genuine game mechanic, not just background atmosphere.
- Prefer playable tabletop, party, solo, and cooperative games without a dedicated app or constant screen usage.
- A standard podcast player on an iPhone should be sufficient for as many concepts as possible; consider small, optionally downloadable files and limited cellular data.
- Explore random seeking, timed announcements, audio clues, changing narrators, sound-based hidden information, recorded oracles, and interactive-feeling mechanics built from fixed audio.
- Be critical about feasibility and limitations: fixed audio is not dynamically interactive, and different podcast players handle seeking, speed, silence, and buffering differently.
- Use entirely original or properly licensed audio and mechanics. A small offline-capable companion web page is possible when necessary, but shouldn't be assumed.
- Begin by brainstorming varied *playable* concepts, studying any instructive precedents, and specifying how each game actually works at the table.
- Don't merge this with the existing utility podcast plan or create a repository until we have selected a direction.
