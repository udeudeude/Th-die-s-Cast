# Future research: companion-app download/storage sizes

_Status: future study only. The user asked for a **full list at a later date**, not a speculative or incomplete list now._

The podcast-only premise partly exists because large official companion apps can be inconvenient to keep installed and expensive to download at game night. Eventually publish a sourced, date-stamped cross-platform survey of the official apps for games mentioned in our planning.

## Research methodology

- Measure iPhone App Store listed **download size**, whether the publisher has separate on-demand audio downloads, and actual reported **installed storage use** if reliably available. These are different measurements.
- Measure Google Play downloaded file size / device-specific install estimates only where current, reliable information exists. Do not equate Android app-bundle download numbers and post-install footprint.
- Record platform, source URL, publisher, region, version, observation date, and whether companion download is mandatory to play.
- Include app-free solutions where available, but remember Th’die’s Cast itself remains **podcast-only** with no web app.

## Candidate survey

One Night / International One Night; Escape: The Curse of the Temple; FUSE; 5-Minute Dungeon; 5-Minute Marvel; 5-Minute Mystery; Flatline; Project: ELITE; Space Alert; XCOM: The Board Game; Stop Thief!; other app-assisted games as identified. For games without apps, record 'no required companion app' rather than guessing a size.

**First verified example (as of 2026-10-09):** One Night, Bezier Games, US iOS App Store, **148.5 MB** listed size. Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-night/id728175611 . Size is subject to change, and App Store listing size is not guaranteed to equal installed footprint.
