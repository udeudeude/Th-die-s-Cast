# Instructions for agents maintaining Th’die’s Cast

**Before claiming podcast audio cannot be produced:** Read [docs/AUDIO_PRODUCTION_RUNBOOK.md](docs/AUDIO_PRODUCTION_RUNBOOK.md). The working Kokoro + Lewis voice/audio studio runs on **GitHub Actions in `udeudeude/udeudeude.github.io`**. Local containers may not contain Kokoro, but that does not prevent building episodes using the existing remote workflows.

The editorial project is this repository; the final MP3s, RSS, scripts, and Actions workflows live in `udeudeude/udeudeude.github.io/thdies-cast`.

Follow the approved Brother Rubicon voice treatment and spoken title, check prior workflow runs and preserved artifacts before repeating audio production, and verify the public feed and Pages deployment before reporting success. Keep audience-facing descriptions literary and functional, not technical production notes.

**Current editorial requirements:** 80 chime-free encounters in each of Creatures Behaving Strangely and Wilderness Oracle; brief natural pauses; no generated separator noises. Compare spoken opening/coda loudness against spoken results on each dice recording. Preserve all 14 episode identities and the timer's exact timing. Do not rerun the obsolete five-episode pilot generator against the current published feed.
